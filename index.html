How to Get Started

1. Create

Click **Create** on the landing page to generate a new chat room. You will be provided with a unique 6-character code.

2. Join

Share the 6-character key with your participants. Others can join by clicking **Join** and entering the valid key.

3. Expiry

To ensure privacy, all chat rooms are permanently deleted 24 hours after creation. Communicate within the timeframe.
